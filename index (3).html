About

I started in electronics: building LoRa links, gas detectors and IoT systems, then working on drone and counter-drone projects. That work showed me how much of modern security depends on connected technology, and how many of its weak points sit in radios, sensors and ground stations.

I now want to apply that understanding to security operations. My exposure so far includes an internship with The Shipping Corporation of India, NCC leadership training, and certified study of OSINT, data protection law and mobile security.

My interest is in security that keeps essential operations running, not only security that guards a gate.
